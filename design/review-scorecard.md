## Key fixes by round
- **Round 1 → 2:** new bottom navigation; full sell journey (quote, BI, proposal, issuance); search by policy number; tappable tiles; console screens for onboarding, compliance and commission; customer journey.
- **Round 2 → 3:** missing items shown on proposal steps; three status chips on proposals with a distinct “unknown” state; lapsed vs grace colours; labelled AI abstentions; reasons for disabled AI skills; one-tap call and WhatsApp on leads.
- **Round 4:** full CRM workspace (leads, lead record, pipeline, customers, tasks, campaigns, routing, import and duplicates) plus mobile lead record and tasks.
- **Round 5 → 6:** collapsible console sidebar; language remembered across agent screens; breadcrumbs; setup card on Today; plan-limit warnings; plain-language integration errors; role-edit locks and preview; report insights; rule-based next best actions; drive pace forecast.
- **Round 7:** consolidated proposal form with readiness meter, source chips, section confirm, next-to-finish, insurer switch reusing answers, customer-only health section; proposal desk for portal entry; question bank with insurer mapping and form-version control.
