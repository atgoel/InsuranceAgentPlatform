Proposal form architecture (extends Revision 3.0 F16 and the data model)

Three layers. Core questions asked once per person (identity, contact, occupation, income, tax residency, nominee, payer); product packs (term, savings, health per member, motor); insurer-only extras. Each insurer form version maps every field to a canonical key, a field code and a value mapping.
Storage. Answers are facts on the party with source, confirmer and time. A proposal freezes a versioned JSON snapshot mapped to the chosen insurer form version, matching the document’s rule of versioned JSON for insurer answers with relational keys elsewhere.
Least effort. Prefill from Customer 360, ID scans (F49), needs analysis, held and previous policies, household; confirm whole sections; jump to the next unfinished item; switching insurer reuses answers and asks only the delta; insurer rules shown inline.
Who answers. Health and lifestyle declarations are answered and confirmed only by the customer (link or hand-over with OTP). Salespeople and AI never type them. F52 proposal AI stays later scope.
Output. One mapped snapshot becomes the API payload (contracted insurers, F18) or the assisted portal fill sheet in the insurer’s field order. Submission keeps the idempotency key; issuance waits for the insurer.
Change control. New insurer form versions are diffed against the live version, mapped, then published with an effective date; open proposals keep their frozen version.
Recommend adding these decisions to the design document as Revision 3.1, and reviewing health-data handling with the CISO and compliance team before the pilot.

States every screen needs in build

Loading skeleton · empty state with next action · error with retry · offline (queued writes, no sensitive files cached) · permission denied by role or tenant · Hindi and pilot-language strings with ICU plurals · low-end Android performance budget.

Rules that must not be lost in build

Issued only on insurer confirmation · reconcile unknown submissions before retry, same idempotency key · AI never fills declarations or quotes premiums · comparison scope from entity type and tie-ups · publish blocked without valid approval reference · suppression rechecked at send time · tenant resolved from verified domain, never from a caller-supplied ID.
