Agent-practitioner review scorecard

Reviewer persona

“Rekha” (fictional composite): 18 years as an individual life agent, then branch manager at an IMF with 60 ISPs and POSPs. Works mostly on a mid-range Android phone, switches between Hindi and English, and judges a tool by one question: does it save me time on the call and keep my renewals alive?

Method and limits

Each round scored every screen 0–10 on the four requested dimensions plus wow / sellability, then averaged. The loop stopped once every dimension exceeded 9. This is a structured self-review by Claude in the persona, not an independent agent or human review. Test with 5–8 real agents on low-end Android before build sign-off.

Round 1 findings → fixes

  • Navigation: no route to leads, calendar or proposals; “Sell” dropped me into one customer’s comparison. → New bottom bar: Today · Leads · Customers · Book · Me; quick actions on Today.
  • Can’t finish a sale: no quote, benefit illustration, proposal or issuance. → Quote & BI, Proposal, Proposals tracker, customer journey.
  • Search: “When a customer calls, I search by policy number first.” → Search on Today and Leads, console search in the sidebar.
  • Dead numbers: Today’s tiles weren’t tappable. → Tiles open the filtered list.
  • Manager blind spots: no onboarding, compliance or commission. → Six console screens behind a grouped sidebar.
  • Customer never seen: → Branded customer journey with insurer payment handoff.

Round 2 findings → fixes

  • Proposal: missing items only surfaced at review. → Step chips show “2 left” / “1 missing” in orange from the start.
  • Proposals list: needed submitted / paid / issued at a glance; “unknown” must not look like “failed”. → Three status chips per card; dashed amber for unknown with a reconcile action.
  • Calendar: lapsed and grace looked alike. → Red for lapsed, amber for grace, sorted by urgency; month-wide filter lists.
  • AI trust: abstentions looked like answers. → Labelled “Abstained” in amber with a route to the insurer quote.
  • AI controls: a switched-off skill gave no reason. → Per-insurer and per-language gate results with fallback notes.
  • Leads: call and WhatsApp were two taps away. → One-tap actions on every card; duplicate resolution inline.

Navigation
  • Console sidebar grew to 22 items → collapsible sections with counts; the current section opens itself.
  • Language reset on every screen → one switch, remembered across agent screens; titles and tabs translate everywhere.
  • Customer record had no way back → breadcrumb CRM › Customers › name.
  • Help and billing were hidden → reachable from Today’s setup card, the avatar and the Me hub.
Ease of use
  • New agents didn’t know where to start → setup progress card on Today.
  • Plan limits surprised users → meters warn at 75%; drafts fall back to templates.
  • Integration errors read like logs → plain-language reasons with Retry or Go manual.
  • Role edits were risky → locked permissions and a “what this role sees” preview.
Wow
  • Reports were bare tables → a “What stands out” line on each report.
  • Customer record was passive → next best actions, labelled as rules because predictive AI is later scope.
  • Drives lacked momentum → pace forecast and a leaderboard switch for the agent app.

Round 4 · stakeholder feedback the persona review missed: CRM depth

Rounds 1–3 judged the agent’s phone journey and under-weighted the CRM that managers, operations and ISPs run all day. Added: leads workspace with SLA timers and bulk assignment, full lead record with stage rules and conversion, opportunities pipeline, customers and households, tasks and cadences, campaigns and segments, routing rules with a test bench, import and duplicate review, plus mobile lead record and task list. Not re-scored; validate with a branch manager and an operations user.

Sellability moments to lead a prospect demo with

1. Book in minutes. Photo of a policy becomes a reviewed held policy and a due date.
2. One switch, four regulatory personas. Comparison scope changes for IMF, broker, POSP and agent.
3. Hindi in one tap. Today’s screen and WhatsApp drafts flip language; voice notes become tasks.
4. Compliance that blocks, not warns. No approval reference, no publish; BI acknowledgement unlocks the proposal.
5. Issued means insurer-confirmed. Paid-not-issued and unknown submissions are tracked and reconciled.
6. Their brand, live. White-label presets re-theme the agent app preview instantly.
