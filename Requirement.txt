
## Core Business Management Features

### Client Relationship Management (CRM)
- **Unified client database** with complete family/household view across life, health, motor, and general insurance 
- **Lead capture and management** from multiple channels (web, mobile, referrals, walk-ins) with automated routing and assignment 
- **Sales pipeline tracking** with stage-wise conversion monitoring and activity logging 
- **Client communication history** with integrated WhatsApp, SMS (DLT-registered), email, and call logging 
- **Customer segmentation** by product type, premium band, renewal date, and profitability 
- **360-degree client view** showing all policies, claims, commissions, and interactions in one screen 
- ** Customer deduplication ** Lead can come in various forms and shape try to match and deduplicate client 

### Policy Administration
- **Multi-insurer policy management** across all lines (life, general, health) with standardized data capture 
- **Quote generation and comparison** with side-by-side product comparison tools 
- **Policy issuance workflow** with proposal form filling, document checklist,document upload, and e-signature integration 
- **Policy lifecycle tracking** from quotation to binding to servicing with complete audit trail 
- **Digital policy delivery** with e-policy storage and DigiLocker integration

### Renewals Management
- **Automated renewal pipeline** with 90/60/30/15-day advance reminders via SMS, WhatsApp, and email 
- **Renewal quotation generation** with one-click renewal proposal creation 
- **Retention tracking dashboard** showing renewal rates, lapsed policies, and recovery opportunities [insuredboard]
- **Bulk renewal processing** for mass communications and batch policy renewals 
- **AI-powered renewal prediction** identifying at-risk renewals based on client behavior patterns
