## Review findings

Reviewer summary: M06 delivers the five calculators, the advice record (draft to finalised, scope snapshot, immutable afterwards, enforced again by a restrictive RLS update policy), the quote workspace with LA-6 scope checks, BI evidence and the signed share link. Routes, permissions, status and error codes match the LLD; record scope follows opportunity or party and fails closed to 404; share tokens use HMAC-SHA256 with timing-safe compare, host-tenant binding and a security log without the token; money is integer paise end to end (bigint with a safe-integer guard) and dates are IST. Gaps: the stale-quote job (expireStale) exists but is never scheduled, BI-required categories PENSION and CHILD have no test, and the web gate runner reports FAIL even though the vitest run of the same paths passes.
